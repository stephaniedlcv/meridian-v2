MERIDIAN — LABS EXPLANATION INTELLIGENCE AUDIT + REBUILD (HIGH PRIORITY)

We are now entering a refinement/polish phase for Meridian Labs before external presentation/testing.

The infrastructure, ingestion flow, panel grouping, and rendering are already functioning correctly.

The current weak point is the explanation intelligence layer.

PROBLEM:
Many biomarkers are still falling back to generic explanation copy.

Right now, multiple markers display nearly identical text such as:
“This biomarker is one of the signals Meridian tracks over time…”

This breaks immersion and reduces trust because different biomarkers should feel meaningfully different.

GOAL:
Every biomarker explanation should feel:
- specific
- intelligent
- clinically grounded
- emotionally calm
- warm but not overly casual
- easy to understand without sounding simplistic
- premium and human
- never AI-generic
- never robotic
- never fear-inducing
- never condescending

Meridian should feel like:
“someone intelligent helping you understand your health calmly.”

NOT:
“generic AI-generated health filler.”

==================================================
TASK
==================================================

Perform a FULL audit of the Labs explanation system.

Audit ALL explanation layers:
- Why It Matters
- Meridian Context
- Trend interpretation
- Panel descriptions
- Fallback explanation paths
- History explanations

Identify:
1. biomarkers using generic fallback copy
2. duplicate explanations reused across unrelated biomarkers
3. explanation overlap between “Why It Matters” and “Meridian Context”
4. tone inconsistencies
5. biomarkers missing contextual specificity
6. explanations that sound AI-generated or vague
7. explanations that are too clinical/jargony
8. explanations that sound emotionally cold or alarming

==================================================
REBUILD RULES
==================================================

WHY IT MATTERS:
- explain what the biomarker represents biologically
- explain why humans care about it
- explain what system/body process it relates to
- use plain but intelligent language
- should feel personal and clinically literate
- should help users understand the “purpose” of the marker

MERIDIAN CONTEXT:
- should NOT repeat Why It Matters
- should explain:
  - patterns
  - relationships
  - trends
  - contextual interpretation
  - why Meridian evaluates it alongside other markers
- should feel more “systems-thinking”
- should reinforce longitudinal analysis

TREND LANGUAGE:
- supportive
- emotionally intelligent
- encouraging when improving
- calm/non-alarmist when worsening
- never shame the user
- never catastrophize
- acknowledge effort subtly when appropriate

==================================================
TONE REQUIREMENTS
==================================================

The tone is EXTREMELY IMPORTANT.

The user should feel:
- informed
- respected
- calm
- understood
- guided

The app should NEVER sound:
- like a textbook
- like ChatGPT
- like a hospital portal
- like wellness influencer fluff
- emotionally flat
- emotionally dramatic

Avoid:
- “This marker indicates…”
- “This means you have…”
- “This is bad…”
- “This is dangerous…”

Prefer:
- “This marker helps reflect…”
- “Changes here can sometimes overlap with…”
- “Meridian watches this over time because…”
- “Patterns here may provide context around…”
