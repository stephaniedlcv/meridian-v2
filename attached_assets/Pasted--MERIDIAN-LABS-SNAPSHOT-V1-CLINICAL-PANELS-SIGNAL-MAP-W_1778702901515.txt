- Vitamin & Nutrient Panel

- Other

Important:
Clinical Panels should feel medical/clinical and recognizable.
Do not use wellness-style names here.

Marker mapping should use existing marker_name / slug logic already available in the page where possible.

hemoglobin, hematocrit, rbc, wbc, platelets, mcv, mch, mchc, rdw, neutrophils, lymphocytes, monocytes, eosinophils, basophils
