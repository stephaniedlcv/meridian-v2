PROBLEM:
After a user uploads a lab PDF, reviews/confirms markers, and saves them, Meridian shows a success screen with actions like uploading another PDF or returning home.

For users uploading multiple lab PDFs from the same routine lab visit, this is too slow and disruptive.

GOAL:
Make the post-save success state faster and more useful for multi-PDF uploads.

Expected UX:
After successful save, show a compact success confirmation that allows the user to:
1. Close the success state and remain on Labs
2. Upload another PDF immediately
3. View the updated Labs Snapshot
