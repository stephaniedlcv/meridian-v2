TASK:
Implement Range Bar Direction V1.

CURRENT ISSUE:
All biomarker range bars use the same gradient logic, as if every biomarker works the same way:

low risk / danger → optimal middle → high risk / danger

But biomarkers do not all behave the same.

Meridian needs range bar visuals that respect marker interpretation direction.

PRODUCT PRINCIPLE:
The range bar should communicate the correct biological interpretation.

Not all markers are “middle is best.”

There are at least three visual interpretation types:

1. middle_is_best
- low can be unfavorable
- middle/clinical range is favorable
- high can be unfavorable

Examples:
- sodium
- potassium
- chloride
- calcium
- wbc
- rbc
- hemoglobin
- hematocrit
- platelets
- tsh
- many CBC/CMP markers

2. lower_is_better
- lower/controlled is generally favorable
- higher is less favorable
- bar should not imply low is equally bad unless there is a known lower clinical bound

Examples:
- ldl
- triglycerides
- total_cholesterol
- non_hdl
- hba1c
- glucose_fasting
- insulin_fasting
- hs_crp
- homocysteine

3. higher_is_better
- low is less favorable
- adequate/higher within safe context is favorable
- bar should not imply high is bad unless a clinical upper bound exists

Examples:
- hdl
- egfr
- vitamin_d, only within safe upper context
- vitamin_b12, if current product logic supports upper safety context

3. Visual rules

For middle_is_best:
Use existing symmetric style:
low unfavorable → favorable middle → high unfavorable

For lower_is_better:
Use a directional style:
favorable zone starts toward the low/controlled side.
Higher values move toward watch/attention.

Do not make the low side look dangerous unless there is an explicit clinical minimum and the value is below it.

For higher_is_better:
Use a directional style:
low side less favorable
adequate/higher side favorable

Do not make higher values look dangerous unless there is an explicit clinical upper bound and the value exceeds it.

For unknown:
Use a neutral range bar or no range bar.
Prefer no bar over misleading bar.

4. Keep range safety rules

Only render a range bar when:
- value is finite
- reference_range_min and reference_range_max are finite
- reference_range_min < reference_range_max

Do not invent ranges.
Do not render fake/blank bars.

5. Labels

For higher_is_better:
Labels may be:

or Low / High if already used and visually safe.
