CURRENT ISSUE:
Phase 1 improved action_steps, but vague/overly strong wording can still appear in headline/status/cause.

Observed example:
status:
“eGFR at 84 suggests filtration efficiency is worth optimizing today.”

cause:
“...it's worth supporting kidney function with simple adjustments today.”

This is not ideal because:
- “optimizing” is vague
- “supporting kidney function” sounds too clinical and may make the user think something is wrong with their kidneys
- the copy still leaves room for interpretation
- Meridian should not sound diagnostic or organ-alarming

1. Add full-field vague language rule

Update the system prompt so these words are discouraged across all user-facing fields unless immediately explained with a concrete behavior:

- optimize
- optimizing
- support
- supporting
- balance
- balancing
- monitor
- monitoring
- moderate

Important:
They are especially discouraged when paired with organs or body systems.

Bad:
“support kidney function”
“optimize filtration efficiency”
“balance electrolytes”
“monitor symptoms”

Better:
“keep fluids steady”
“watch this pattern over time”

2. Add organ-language caution

Add a rule:
Avoid phrases that imply an organ is failing, struggling, overloaded, or needs support.

Avoid:
- “support kidney function”
- “kidney workload”
- “your kidneys are…”
- “liver support”
- “your liver is struggling”
- “your body isn’t clearing”

Preferred:
- “filtration marker”
- “kidney-related marker”
- “this lab signal”
- “fluid balance signal”
- “today’s priority is keeping fluids steady”

3. Improve status/cause examples

Add examples to the prompt.

Bad:
“eGFR at 84 suggests filtration efficiency is worth optimizing today.”

Better:
“eGFR at 84 may reflect a temporary shift in hydration, recent intake, or workload. Today’s priority is keeping fluids steady.”

Bad:
“It's worth supporting kidney function with simple adjustments today.”

Better:
“This is not a red flag in this context. Keep fluids steady and avoid unusually high-protein meals today.”

Bad:
“Balance electrolytes.”

Better:
“Keep sodium and potassium intake steady through normal meals today.”

- “Hydration-related marker needs attention”
