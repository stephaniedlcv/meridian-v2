PROBLEM
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Current trend messaging is too cold and mechanical.

Example:
“Down 33 since last result.”

This wastes the emotional and behavioral context of health improvement.

Users uploading labs are often:
- making lifestyle changes
- taking medications consistently
- improving sleep
- exercising
- changing diet
- trying hard to improve their health

Meridian should acknowledge meaningful improvement
WITHOUT:
- sounding childish
- sounding overly celebratory
- sounding emotionally manipulative
- making causal medical claims

CRITICAL RULES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. NEVER CLAIM CAUSALITY

Do NOT say:
- “because of your diet”
- “your exercise fixed this”
- “your treatment worked”
- “you reversed X”

Use:
- “may reflect”
- “can sometimes be seen”
- “is often associated with”
- “may suggest”
- “can occur when”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2. IMPROVEMENTS SHOULD FEEL VALIDATING

When markers improve meaningfully:

DO:
- acknowledge positive movement
- recognize sustained effort indirectly
- reinforce trend significance calmly

DO NOT:
- overhype
- celebrate excessively
- use emojis
- sound like a fitness coach

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
GOOD EXAMPLE — IMPROVEMENT

“This marker has improved meaningfully since your last upload (-33). Sustained shifts like this are often seen when recovery, metabolic health, lifestyle consistency, or treatment adherence are moving in a healthier direction.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
3. WORSENING TRENDS MUST FEEL SAFE

If a marker worsens:
DO NOT:
- shame
- catastrophize
- trigger panic
- imply disease progression
- sound emotionally negative

Avoid:
- “bad”
- “dangerous”
- “failing”
- “declining health”
- “concerning” unless clinically necessary

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
GOOD EXAMPLE — WORSENING

“This marker moved away from its previous range since your last upload. Temporary shifts can happen from stress, illness, inflammation, sleep disruption, hydration changes, medications, recovery load, or evolving metabolic patterns. Meridian will continue watching whether this is isolated or part of a broader trend.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
4. DISTINGUISH:
ISOLATED VS PERSISTENT

This is critical.

Meridian should differentiate between:
- one-off fluctuation
- repeated directional pattern

Examples:

ISOLATED:
“This appears more consistent with a temporary fluctuation than a long-term pattern.”

PERSISTENT:
“This marker has remained outside its usual range across multiple uploads, which may make the trend more meaningful than an isolated deviation.”

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
5. MERIDIAN SHOULD SOUND OBSERVATIONAL

Tone should feel:
- intelligent
- calm
- analytical
- humane
- emotionally safe

NOT:
- motivational
- dramatic
- sterile
- corporate

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
6. DO NOT REMOVE NUMERIC DATA

Keep:
- delta values
- previous date

But enrich the interpretation layer around them.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
7. NEVER OVERSTATE SMALL CHANGES

Tiny fluctuations should be framed carefully.

Example:
“Minor movement from the prior result, though still within the type of variation commonly seen between lab draws.”
