Meridian currently mixes:

1. Source lab panels
AND
2. Biological system categories

inside the same History presentation layer.

This creates confusion.

Example problems already detected:

- MPV and Platelets appearing under “Other” instead of CBC
- CMP biomarkers being split into:
  - Liver
  - Kidney/Renal
  - etc.
- Thyroid markers grouped under “Thyroid” instead of preserving their original source panel structure
- Vitamins grouped directly into “Vitamins & Nutrients”

The biological grouping itself is NOT wrong.

The problem is:
## Source panel structure is being lost in the default History view.
